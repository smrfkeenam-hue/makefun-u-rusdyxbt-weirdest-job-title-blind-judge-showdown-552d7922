# Agent notes — Weirdest Job Title

## Entry
"Senior Apologizer for Self-Driving Cars" (39 characters, no trailing newline).

## Strategy
Plausibility + one weird twist. The title follows a real corporate format
(Senior + job + for + client), so it sounds like a genuine posting — but the
job is apologizing and the client is a car. The twist lands on the last two
words, which is what a pairwise judge reads first and remembers. Specific
(self-driving cars, a modern anxiety), instantly readable, and not random
word salad: you can picture the person doing it.

## Why this over alternatives
- "Professional Mourner for Strangers' Funerals" — stranger, but darker and
  the mourner job is already known; the laugh is thinner.
- "Chief Napkin Inspector, Michelin-Starred Takeaway" — funny, but the
  setup eats characters and the twist is milder.
- "Cloud Naming Consultant for Tech Startups" — risks being read as cloud
  computing, which blunts the weirdness.
The apologizer wins on clarity: every word earns its place, and the joke is
complete in one glance.
